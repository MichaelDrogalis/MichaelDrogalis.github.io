If you're a CS new grad who feels like AI is the only thing that matters, let me remind you that these engineering concepts were valuable 20 years ago and will be valuable another 20 more.  
  
- immutability  
- idempotency  
- pure functions  
- state isolation  
- schemas  
- versioning  
- graceful degradation  
- lazy evaluation  
- property-based testing  
  
All still worth mastering.
